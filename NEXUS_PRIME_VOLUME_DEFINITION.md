## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

In a sustainable multi-level network marketing ecosystem, gross customer payments, distributor wholesale prices, and qualification volumes serve fundamentally different operational purposes. The Nexus Prime Volume Engine explicitly decouples financial payment amounts from qualification and commissionable volume metrics to prevent arithmetic distortions and ensure compliance with corporate governance.

## 2. Core Volume Categorization

```
                               ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                               │   Gross Order Amount (LKR)  │
                               └──────────────┬──────────────┘
                                              │ (Volume Mapping Ratio)
                                              ▼
                               ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                               │     Product Volume (PV)     │
                               └───────┬─────────────┬───────┘
                                       │             │
                    ┌──────────────────┘             └──────────────────┐
                    ▼                                                   ▼
       ┌─────────────────────────┐                         ┌─────────────────────────┐
       │   Personal Volume (PV)  │                         │    Team Volume (TV)     │
       └─────────────────────────┘                         └─────────────────────────┘
        (Orders by Distributor)                             (Downline Network Tree)
```

### 2.1 Gross Order Amount (LKR)
- **Definition:** The total liquid fiat currency in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) billed and paid by the customer/distributor for an order (inclusive of discounts and applicable taxes).
- **Source of Truth:** `nexus_orders.total` where `payment_status = 'paid'`.
- **Purpose:** Financial accounting, double-entry wallet ledger debit/credit, and payment gateway settlement.

### 2.2 Product Volume / Qualification Volume (PV)
- **Definition:** The standardized numerical volume points assigned to an item or order for rank qualification purposes.
- **Default Baseline Mapping:** $1.00\text{ LKR} = 1.00\text{ Volume Point}$ unless a custom SKU/package volume point mapping is configured in the product catalog.
- **Status:** `CONFIGURATION REQUIRED` for production point-to-fiat conversion ratios.

### 2.3 Commissionable Volume (CV)
- **Definition:** The specific portion of an order's value upon which multi-level commission percentages are calculated.
- **Relationship:** Depending on corporate margin policy, $\text{CV}$ may equal $100\%$ of order value or a configured percentage per product line.
- **Status:** `CONFIGURATION REQUIRED`.

### 2.4 Personal Volume (PV)
- **Definition:** The cumulative qualifying volume generated by a distributor through their **own** verified orders within the evaluation period.
- **Calculation Formula:**
  $$\text{PV} = \sum_{\substack{\text{orders} \in \text{Member Orders} \\ \text{status} \in \{\text{'paid'}, \text{'completed'}\} \\ \text{created\_at} \in \text{Period}}} \text{Order Total} \times \text{Volume Ratio}$$
- **Strict Inclusion Rules:**
  - `status = 'paid'` OR `status = 'completed'`
  - `payment_status = 'paid'`
- **Strict Exclusion Rules (Zero Volume):**
  - Orders in status `awaiting_payment`, `pending`, `cancelled`, `failed`, or `refunded`.

### 2.5 Team Volume (TV)
- **Definition:** The aggregate volume generated by all qualifying distributors residing in the distributor's downline network tree within the evaluation period.
- **Calculation Formula:**
  $$\text{TV} = \sum_{\substack{\text{member} \in \text{Downline Network} \\ \text{orders} \in \text{Member Orders} \\ \text{status} \in \{\text{'paid'}, \text{'completed'}\} \\ \text{created\_at} \in \text{Period}}} \text{Order Total} \times \text{Volume Ratio}$$
- **Traversal Mechanism:** Efficiently traverses the `nexus_network_closure` table.
- **Depth Constraints:** Supports lifetime depth or configurable maximum depth limits (e.g. Levels 1–5).

### 2.6 Direct Group Volume (DGV)
- **Definition:** The volume generated exclusively by the distributor's **Level 1** direct referrals (depth distance $= 1$).
- **Purpose:** Enables leadership qualification criteria requiring frontline team balance.

## 3. Evaluation Periods & Windows

| Period Type | Description | Boundary Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| `lifetime` | All historical qualifying activity since registration | $\text{Start} = \text{Registration Date}$, $\text{End} = \text{Now}$ |
| `monthly` | Activity within the current calendar month | $\text{Start} = \text{1st of Month 00:00:00}$, $\text{End} = \text{Last of Month 23:59:59}$ |
| `weekly` | Activity within the current Monday–Sunday cycle | ISO week boundaries in server timezone |
| `rolling_period` | Dynamic sliding window (e.g. past 30 or 60 days) | $\text{Start} = \text{Now} - N\text{ days}$, $\text{End} = \text{Now}$ |
| `custom_period` | Arbitrary promotional or qualification campaign window | Configured start and end timestamps |

## 4. Reversals, Refunds & Order Cancellation Handling

- If a previously paid order is refunded, cancelled, or reversed:
  1. The order status updates to `refunded` or `cancelled`.
  2. The server-side rank qualification engine detects the change during scheduled or triggered evaluation.
  3. The volume is automatically excluded from current period metrics.
  4. If rank demotion is enabled in corporate settings, the member's qualification is adjusted with an audit trail. If demotion is disabled (default), the distributor retains their historical highest achieved rank.
