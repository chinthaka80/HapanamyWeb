Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries about the Nexus Prime platform.

What is Nexus Prime? +
Nexus Prime (PVT) Ltd is a digital commerce and professional network marketing platform designed to provide practical business curriculums and structured team distribution opportunities.
How do I become a member? +
You can register online by visiting the registration page and providing your details along with a verified sponsor referral code.
What is a referral code? +
A referral code connects you to the distributor who introduced you to Nexus Prime, ensuring correct placement in the network structure.
How does the network structure work? +
Members build teams hierarchically through direct invitations. The platform uses deterministic graph technology to track relationships and team depth accurately.
Where can I see my team? +
Once signed in to your Member Dashboard, you can view your team via both the interactive Network Tree and the Team Directory.
Where can I see commissions? +
Eligible commissions and referral rewards are tracked in the Commissions section of your protected member dashboard.
How do withdrawals work? +
When your verified wallet balance meets the minimum threshold, you may submit a withdrawal request to your registered bank account for processing.
Can I change my sponsor? +
To protect network integrity, sponsor relationships are immutable once assigned during registration. Exceptions require formal administrative compliance review.
How do I contact support? +
You can reach support through the Contact Us form on this website or by submitting a ticket directly inside your member dashboard.
